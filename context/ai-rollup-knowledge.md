## Investor Sentiment Report 2026 (Tenet survey, N=102; 79% VC, 13% PE, 8% FO; 50% Europe)
- ~50% call AI roll-ups a new asset class; only ~15% "truly VC-scale"; rest "PE with AI wrapper"/none.
- Tenet thesis: **"PE floor with a VC ceiling."**
- Appetite peaks **after 1–2 proof-of-concept acquisitions**; gap at pre-acquisition stage → Tenet proposes merging inception + pre-acquisition into a larger first round.
- Value levers: AI-led margin 86%, multiple expansion 46%, AI-led topline 32%.
- EBITDA bar: >90% accept 2x uplift as viable; Tenet internal simulation: **~3x uplift ≈ threshold for VC-funded**; 2x → better suited to PE-style capital structure. Traditional PE roll-up levers cap at ~10–15% EBITDA uplift.
- Risks: integration/change mgmt 79%, overhyped AI value 68%, poor M&A discipline #3.
- Exits: ~80% expect sale to PE consolidators; 30%+ IPO for breakouts; also HoldCos (Constellation-style), strategics.
- Quotes: Dwelly founder (fell "between the cracks": VCs send to PE, PE won't do tech); Buena founder (burned $14m, no PMF, pivoted to M&A as GTM); Zinco founder David Alonso Martinez (integrate within 60 days); soundbites incl. "returns, but not the kind VCs are underwriting", "Rocket Internet model of the GenAI era", "minority model won't hit return curve unless owning 30%+".

## Financing (essays 2, 9 + model)
- First acquisition: mostly **equity + seller notes/earn-outs**; lenders avoid unproven model.
- Venture debt once integrated; lenders comfortable ~**$10M revenue + group EBITDA breakeven**; later **50–70% of acquisition value debt-funded**; debt ≈ **≥ half of lifetime capital**.
- Lenders **haircut or ignore unproven AI uplift**; credit only uplift repeated across assets / visible in P&L.
- Structure: TopCo/HoldCo – MidCo (platform) – OpCo/AcquiCo per target; debt placed near cash flows (MidCo/AcquiCo).
- Binding constraint usually **interest coverage / liquidity**, not Debt/EBITDA. Venture-debt warrants ≈5% dilution, up to 15–20% in structured deals. Raise takes 4–5 months; costs 2–4% of facility. Refinancing into larger/cheaper facilities "expected" as track record grows (→ links to Ancora "ricapitalizzare").
- Tenet model defaults: equity share of EV 35% (1st deal) → 30% → 25% → 15% (4th+); rest bank debt (10%), vendor loan (7.5%), earn-out (24m); entry 5.5x EV/EBITDA; uplift 2.5x in 9 months; 3–4 equity rounds total, 25–30% dilution each.
- Essay 3 (VC-scale): 10x for seed needs **4–5x EBITDA uplift**, fast; sub-linear HQ cost; **no reliance on multiple expansion**; <$50M equity to reach ~$70M EBITDA in 10y (model, not evidence).
- LTV/CAC essay: CAC = EV×1.1; with 50% COGS automation LTV/CAC ≈ 10.9x; 3x only if paying ~10x EBITDA, churn 14%, or no AI uplift. Claims "based on actual results across 100+ acquisitions" — unverified.

## Acquisition guide (essay 12)
- <€2m EBITDA European services: low/mid single-digit EV/EBITDA; size discount ≥1 turn between €0.5m and €2m EBITDA (= classic buy-and-build arbitrage).
- Owner-replacement add-down is the biggest adjustment; add-backs >20–30% of EBITDA = red flag; write EBITDA basis into LOI; disputed value → earn-out.

## Industry selection (essays 1, 5 + screener)
- Good sectors: fragmented, low software adoption ("software aversion"), manual workflows, AI automates core service, recurring revenue, stable demand, product add-on potential.
- Roll-up vs organic: cyclicality, distribution stickiness, service standardisation, regulation (regulated + sticky → buy).

## Bending Spoons — Mostly Metrics F-1 breakdown (Gustafson, 11 Jun 2026; read in full)
Secondary source; cite the F-1 for numbers where possible.
- 50+ acquisitions since 2013, **never sold one**; every deal underwritten as hold-forever.
- Revenue FY25 $1.31bn (+95%); **organic 13%** (7% in 2024) → ~82 of 95 points bought. Q1-26 $601M (+132%). NRR ~94% (Evernote 99, AOL 95, StreamYard 91, Remini 87).
- GAAP net income FY25 ≈ $0; adjusted net income $376M. Add-backs $335M > GAAP op. income $278M (amortisation $151M, transaction $85M, **reorganisation $79M — recurring by design**, other $21M). Adj. op. margin ~47%.
- **Financing:** 2025 operating cash flow $291M vs $1.85bn invested (acquisitions); gap filled by $1.94bn financing. Q1-26: $1.65bn deployed, $1.74bn raised. ~$4.6bn deployed 2023–Q1 26. Debt $4.36bn, cash $741M. Interest $143M FY25 (+337%) = 51% of op. income; Q1-26 $93M = 78%.
- Equity marks: $2.8bn (~2024) → $11.7bn (Goldman-led $710M, Oct 2025) → ~$20bn IPO target (Nasdaq BSP, ~$1.5bn raise). Dual-class, founders control. Largest outside holder Galileo Quattordici; Baillie Gifford.
- Shared stack deployed into every acquisition: Pico/Lumen/Abacus (data), Minerva (LTV model), Juno (payments), Janus/Orion (A/B testing) → codified integration (Zollo & Singh angle).
- AI: AI-authored PRs <10% (Q1-25) → >90% (Q1-26); revenue/employee $1.12M (2023) → $2.57M (2025). Evernote team 341 → 60 (−82%).
- Red flags: material weaknesses in controls (worsened by acquisition pace), app-store dependency, no long-term contracts, foreign private issuer.
- Author labels it "conglomerate? holding company?", compares to Teledyne/Capital Cities/Constellation/Berkshire; values as sum of parts.
- No dividends or distributions mentioned: cash is reinvested; investor liquidity = private round step-ups + IPO.
