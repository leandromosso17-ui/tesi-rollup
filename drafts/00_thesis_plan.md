## 1. What to change from the brief (and why)

1. **Shop Circle is now Circeus** (rebranded June 2026, EBRD equity, 18 acquisitions). Update Table 1 and the case.
2. **The case design confounds stage with asset type.** Buena buys *services* firms; Circeus and Bending Spoons buy
   *software*. Software holdcos (Constellation, Bending Spoons) existed long before GenAI — they cannot tell you
   what AI changed. The AI question lives in **services roll-ups** (property management, accounting/tax).
   Recommended design: 
   - **Primary cases (AI-enabled services roll-ups, Europe):** property management (Buena vs hallo theo — you already
     have access to one side) and accounting/tax (Ancora IT, Limetax/Afileon/Taxforce DE, Archipel FR).
   - **Benchmark cases (software acquirers):** Bending Spoons (F-1 = your only hard financial data) and Circeus.
   - **Failure precedent:** Thrasio. Theory benchmark: Constellation.
   This turns "one trajectory across stages" (not defensible: three different businesses) into a 2-axis comparison:
   *what is acquired* (services vs software) x *capital stack* (equity / equity+debt / listed+debt).
3. **Hypotheses -> propositions.** With ~12 interviews and multiple cases you are doing theory-building
   (Eisenhardt 1989), not testing. Call them P1–P3 and say what evidence would contradict each. Sola will push on this.
4. **H3 likely needs reframing.** Your own data already points elsewhere: hallo theo funds acquisitions with
   credit funds, vendor loans and earn-outs, and raises equity for *product and organic growth*. Archipel (€50m equity
   + €65m Eurazeo debt), Numica (equity + growth-debt facility from year one), Dwelly ($263m equity+debt) look the same.
   Emerging proposition: the equity/debt split is set **by use of proceeds from day one** (equity = platform,
   debt = acquired cash flows), not sequentially by stage. That is a sharper, more original finding than the brief's H3.
5. **H2 (integration learning curve) is hard to evidence by Jan.** Time-to-migration data will not come from
   interviews reliably. Keep it, but test it only on Bending Spoons' deal chronology (pre/post-2023 acquisitions)
   plus operator claims, and state it as the weakest-evidenced proposition.
