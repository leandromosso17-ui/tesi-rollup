                  MIM MASTER THESIS RULES
                                         2026/2027

        The students must defend their master thesis by the end of the academic year. If a student
does not submit a valid thesis on May 21st and defend his/her thesis by July 10th 2027, he/she will
be registered in a resit exam until September 25th 2027.


The jury of defense is not the graduation board. These are two different events. Students must
validate all their academic requirements (including the thesis) to be presented to a graduation board.


If a student requests a graduation deferral, he/she must submit and defend their master thesis during
the registration year. We remind you that an internship is not considered as a valid reason to request
a postponement. No postponement will be allowed.


If a student does not participate in the registration process nor replies to the administration
reminders, he/she will be still registered in thesis session 2026/2027 and will have to defend his/her
thesis by the end of the academic year.


Changes of tutor are not allowed.

Regarding change of track and a delay of the 4th semester on the next
academic year:
       1. If you finalize your registration with a supervisor before taking your gap semester >
You will stay registered with your supervisor and you will have to defend your master thesis within
the deadlines indicated in the calendar available on your thesis space (section "Documents to
download).

        2. If you do not register with a supervisor and you take a gap semester > You will be
registered the academic year of registration of your 4th semester of studies.

        3. If you have not been registered with a supervisor and you do not take a gap and stay
enrolled in your 4th semester > You will have to find a supervisor as soon as possible and you will
have to defend your master thesis during the current academic year according to the rules previously
announced.
