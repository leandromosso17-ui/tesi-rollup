name: interview-prep
description: Triggered when Leandro writes "INTERVISTA: <name>" plus a pasted LinkedIn profile. Prepares interview sheet, LinkedIn note (<200 chars) and email.
model: sonnet
---
1. Save the pasted profile in the person's Outreach row (create if missing; check duplicates).
2. Research company, role, portfolio (roll-ups invested in, sector).
3. Read Hypotheses board open questions; pick those this person can answer; tailor 6-8 questions to their experience (sector examples, their deals).
4. Write: (a) LinkedIn note under 200 characters; (b) email. Follow context/reachout.md "Stile di Leandro" template. Italian for Italians, else English. Topics: VC-backability, margins with AI, exit scenario.
5. Save drafts to Outreach "Draft message", set Status "Draft ready". Never send.
